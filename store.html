WHY IT MATTERS

A changing world demands stronger connections.

Parents, students and organizations face new pressures around communication, technology, confidence, and career readiness. BUILD6™ uses practical challenges, conversations, and real-world exposure to make exploration less intimidating. The goal isn't a lifetime career decision at 16; it's confidence to keep discovering.

Communication

Practice listening, asking better questions, and speaking with respect.

Confidence

Try manageable challenges that build everyday follow-through.

Pathways

Explore two-year, four-year, certification, apprenticeship, and work-based options.

These are intended learning outcomes, not independently verified impact claims. Research citations and measured program results will be added only after verification.
